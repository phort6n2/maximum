3. Advertising identifiers and analytics we capture automatically

Most visitors reach this site by clicking one of our ads. When that happens the advertising platform adds an identifier to the web address, and we read and keep it so we can tell which advertising actually produced an enquiry. Specifically, we capture whichever of the following are present:

  • Google click identifiers — gclid, gbraid, wbraid, gclsrc
  • Other platform click identifiers — msclkid (Microsoft), fbclid (Meta), ttclid (TikTok), li_fat_id (LinkedIn)
  • Campaign parameters — utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term, utm_content
  • Page context — the page you landed on, the page you submitted from, and the referring website if your browser sent one

These are stored in your browser's session storage — not in a long-lived cookie — which means they persist while you browse this site and are discarded when you close the tab. If you submit the form, they are sent along with it so we can attribute the enquiry.

Google tag

We load Google's tag (gtag.js) to measure ad conversions and, where configured, site analytics. Google may set cookies in your browser to do this. Google's handling of that data is governed by its own privacy policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Enhanced conversions — what actually gets sent

When you submit the form we use a Google Ads feature called enhanced conversions. Your email address and phone number are hashed in your browser before they are transmitted — Google's tag applies a one-way cryptographic hash locally, so the plain-text values are not sent to Google by this mechanism. The hashes let Google match the conversion to the ad click that produced it. We do not use this feature to send Google your name, address, vehicle or VIN.

4. Call tracking and dynamic phone numbers

Some phone numbers displayed on this site are tracking numbers that may change between visitors. This is how we tell which advertising generated a phone call. When you call a tracking number, our call platform records call metadata — the number you dialled, your caller ID, the date, time and duration of the call, and which advertising source your visit was attributed to — and connects you to us.

The number shown beside our motor vehicle repair registration in the footer is our registered business line and is deliberately not a tracking number.

Florida is an all-party consent state for call recording (Fla. Stat. § 934.03). If any call is being recorded, you will be told at the start of that call and you may decline.

A note on ad-measurement "sharing"

Passing advertising identifiers and hashed contact details to Google for conversion measurement is treated as sharing for targeted advertising under several state privacy laws, even though no money changes hands. Whether or not a statute obliges us to, we will honour an opt-out — see section 8, or contact us using the details there and we will action it.

6. Phone, text and email contact

By submitting the quote form you agree that we may contact you by phone, text message and email about your enquiry, your quote and your appointment. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text message to opt out of texts, or ask us on a call or by email and we will stop. Opting out of marketing does not stop us contacting you about a job already in progress.

7. How long we keep it

Attribution identifiers held in your browser are discarded when you close the tab. Enquiries that do not become jobs are retained in our CRM for up to 24 months so we can pick up the conversation if you come back. Records relating to work actually performed — including the estimate, invoice and warranty record — are kept for as long as Florida law requires of a registered motor vehicle repair shop, and for as long as the workmanship warranty on your vehicle could be claimed against.

8. Your privacy rights and choices

Florida's Digital Bill of Rights (Fla. Stat. §§ 501.701–501.722) imposes its duties on very large technology companies, and {{LEGAL_NAME}} does not meet its thresholds — so we will not pretend it binds us. What we do instead is honour the following on request, for any customer, because it is reasonable:

  • Know what personal information we have collected about you, where it came from, why we collected it and who we disclosed it to
  • Access a copy of that information in a portable form
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Delete personal information we hold about you, subject to legal exceptions — we may need to keep repair records the state requires us to retain
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, including sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising as described in section 5
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information — although we do not collect sensitive personal information through this site
  • Not be discriminated against for asking. We will not refuse you service, change your price, or give you a lesser standard of service because you exercised any of these choices

If you are a resident of a state whose comprehensive privacy law does apply to a business of our size and grants you additional rights, we will honour those rights as that law requires.
